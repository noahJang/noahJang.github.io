Summary

Ph.D. candidate at MIT designing energy-efficient cryptographic silicon. Two 28 nm ASICs architected and taped out: a post-quantum authentication engine with on-chip TRNG and side-channel/fault countermeasures (measured in silicon; CICC 2026), and a reconfigurable TFHE-CNN processor for encrypted inference (ISSCC 2027, under review). Prior work spans HBM crosstalk-avoidance coding, ADC-based SerDes receivers, and FHE accelerators. Experienced across the full flow from RTL and physical implementation to PCB-level chip bring-up and silicon evaluation.

Post-Quantum Authentication Engine, 28 nm ASICARPA-H · Fall 2023 – Spring 2025
